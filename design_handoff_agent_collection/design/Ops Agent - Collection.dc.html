A1The agent’s round — a settlement is not a float pickup

BCF-04 takes a float off a barber: one person, one amount, no counterparty who could disagree. This is a different act and it needed its own screens — a shop rather than a person, a number that can point either way, a partial that is normal rather than an error, and a piece of paper at the end.

Direction is the row’s first fact, not its colour. Collect and Hand over are words on every row and on every button, because the agent reads this list on a scooter in the sun and colour is the first thing that goes. Green and amber only reinforce.

The round is ordered by age, not by geography. The oldest uncollected dirham is what the 14-day promise is measured against (slice 2 §2.6), so day 9 sits above day 4 even if it is across town. Route optimisation would quietly re-sort the list against the only deadline the business has.

Hand-over rows have no age, and that is shown rather than faked. Age measures how long a shop has held our money. When we owe them, nothing of ours is late — so the row says how long they have been waiting instead. Same list, two different clocks, both named.

The bag is on the screen. He is walking around with cash and the amount is his personal risk, so it is stated at the top with its cap and its drop-off, split into what he has collected and what he is still carrying to give away. This is the number that makes him choose to go to the office before the last visit.

The confirm is a code, not a checkbox. AGT-03 is the immutable moment, so the friction is placed there and nowhere else: the owner reads out the 4-digit code his own app is showing, which is the same mechanism the shop side already uses. See the note below on what that does and does not prove.

Partial is the same path as full. One field, a keypad, and a Full amount chip. Typing less than the full number is not an error state — it changes one sentence on the screen, which tells him what happens to the rest, in words he can repeat to the owner.

Rejected: an undo. There is none, so the design does not imply one; instead the receipt screen says plainly that a mistake becomes a line on next week’s statement and links to the support case that starts it. Also rejected: a signature on the collect direction — asking a man who has just handed over his cash to sign for it is theatre, and it would let the real proof (his code) look optional.

What stops him recording a collection he didn’t make — both, on different directions. The 4-digit code survives, and it belongs to Collect: the owner reads it out of his app, it is tied to that visit and that amount, and it rotates. It proves the agent was standing in the shop with a consenting owner — which is exactly the fraud in that direction, an agent inventing a collection from his scooter. The signature cannot do that job, because a signature on the agent’s own phone is drawn by whoever is holding the phone.
On Hand over the code proves nothing worth having — the owner being present is not in question, the money reaching him is. So that direction takes the owner’s signature plus a received confirmation in his own app, and the code is not asked for. Neither mechanism covers both directions, which is why both exist.
Still open, and not drawn: an owner with no phone or no signal cannot read out a code. The honest fallback is an ops call, which needs a state on the line (verified_by: code | ops_call) and a person to answer the phone on a Friday evening — I have not designed it. Offline collection has the same gap: the device can queue a settlement, but it cannot verify a code it has never seen, so a queued collection is unverified until it syncs and that has to be visible to Karima. Not drawn at all: no fee, no commission, no bank field, no RIB — the agent never types a number that isn’t cash he can see.
Tables the backend does not have: settlement_visit (line, agent, direction, window, state) — the round is a query over this, not over settlement_line, because one line can take two visits; settlement_receipt (visit, ref, amount, recorded_at, device, geo, verified_by, code_ref or signature blob); and agent_bag (running held total, drops) which the bag strip reads.
