B1Book again, and getting the booking out of the app

Build plan block 1, items 20–21. The BOOK AGAIN button has been on the completed card at BKG-04 and the detail screen at BKG-07 since turn 9 and has never done anything. It now opens BKG-24, which is not the normal booking flow: everything is already chosen, so the screen’s only job is to show what it copied and let you change one thing before confirming. Repeat customers are the whole economics of a barber — making them re-pick a service they have had eleven times is the sort of friction that sends them back to walking in. BKG-25 covers the overflow: calendar, share, and directions, with the deliberate omission of a “copy link” row, since a booking link is personal and there is nobody it should be forwarded to.

B5The waitlist middle — a slot opens and the phone rings

Build plan block 5, item 22. The waitlist has had a write since turn 36 (BKG-19, the ask) and a read since turn 8 on the barber’s side (BWL-01, offering the freed slot). Between them: nothing. The barber could offer a slot to three people and none of them had a screen that told them.

The whole design turns on the offer being a race, and saying so. BKG-26 is sent to three candidates at once, not one at a time — a single-candidate offer sits unanswered for twenty minutes while the chair stays empty, which is the outcome the waitlist exists to prevent. So the countdown is real, first tap wins, and the screen says both things plainly rather than implying the slot is reserved. Losing is therefore a normal outcome and needs a screen that doesn’t feel like a failure: BKG-28 keeps the person on the list and says where they are in it.

One deliberate refusal: no deposit is taken to claim. Asking for 24 DH inside a ten-minute window, from someone who may be on a bus, would lose the slot to a payment sheet. Claiming holds the chair; paying happens in the ordinary flow afterwards, and if they never pay the slot returns to the list.

T14The ten My-Bookings states the app has and the canvas didn't

Every screen here is a live branch in MyBookingsScreen.tsx / MyBookingScreen.tsx that had no drawing. Nothing invented: BKG-32 and BKG-33 are the two sides of booking_free_until (0078) — the deadline as a moment, "free until 13:30 today", never "up to 2 hours before" — and the cancel sheet after it lapses, which is the same sheet as BKG-08 with the one number that changed. BKG-34 is CancelledScreen with refunded: true, read off deposit_holds rather than recomputed, so the receipt says what the ledger did; BKG-35 is the same component with withdrawn: true — no money card, no "changed your mind", because the barber never saw it. BKG-36 is MovedScreen (13a), which fires on the next open via moved_seen_<id>, not on the tap that caused it — he accepts while the app is shut. BKG-37 is DeclinedCard's fallback: alt_starts empty, so the screen scans his real calendar a fortnight forward and the eyebrow changes from KARIM SUGGESTS to NEXT FREE WITH KARIM and ACCEPT to ASK — the honest difference between an offer and a guess. BKG-38 is status === 'no_show', the third chip on the Cancelled tab. BKG-39 mounts UnderReviewStrip (38f) on the detail — a shop vanishing from search must never read as a booking vanishing. BKG-40 is the full-screen Receipt behind the Completed tab's RECEIPT button, and BKG-41 is ListEmptyComponent on the other two tabs, which reads "No completed bookings", not the upcoming copy at BKG-02.

T15⋯ → Report a problem, from the booking itself

The header's ⋯ has been drawn since turn 9 and its second row went nowhere. In the code it is an Alert with three options — Message the barber, Report a problem, Close — and the middle one calls onReport(d.id) into the same ReportProblemScreen (17a) that Help Center opens. Same component, and the bookingId prop is the whole difference.

Entered from here it is locked to this booking. Help Center's version at PRO-14 loads the last 20 completed visits and offers a picker, because it has no idea what you're complaining about. Arriving from the booking, visitId is already the answer: the visit card is fixed, there is no Change, and the reasons are the five in REASONS unchanged. Two consequences worth naming: this is the only door to 17a for a booking that hasn't happened yet (the picker filters on completed_at is not null), so BKG-43 is drawn on tomorrow's booking; and picking I was charged the wrong amount passes deposit_cents || price_cents as the disputed figure, which is why the receipt in BKG-44 can name a number without asking him for one.

The filed screen differs by one row from PRO-03: onBack here returns to the booking, so the button reads BACK TO MY BOOKING rather than DONE, and View case still jumps the thread. Nothing about the booking changes by reporting it — the chair is still yours, and BKG-43 says so on the screen, because a report that reads like a cancellation is a report nobody files.
