T12The switches, and the message they were promised

Where PRO-17's button goes and what PRO-19's toggle eventually sends. PRO-20 divides the list by a rule rather than by category: anything that is the booking cannot be switched off — a cancellation is not a message about your booking, it is your booking — while everything that is an offer is off and has never been asked for. Fourteen messages in fifteen months is the honest headline. PRO-21 is the reopening notice, and it keeps its promise narrowly: one message, no second, his cancellation record shown on the same screen as his free slots, and no discount for August because the refund already happened.

T13Turning it off, and the word we never show you

The two ends of PRO-20. PRO-22 is the switch being turned off, and the design rule is that a confirm screen is allowed to state consequences and not allowed to bargain — it names the two people it silences, says what Hamza will and will not see, and there is no “are you sure you'll miss out”. T15The rumour was true, and the thing worth complaining about

Two closings. PRO-25 is the sighting from EXPL-12 three weeks later, and it is the hardest one to write honestly: she was right — Mehdi was in Mghogha — and we still would not have printed it. What reached her was him joining, not her tip, and the page says the tip helped Hicham decide where to walk rather than pretending it did more.
