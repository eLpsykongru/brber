T3One inbox, two tabs — Clients and Ops

Until now the barber had threads (BMS-01, BMS-02) but no place they lived. Clients and Ops share one inbox because a barber checks his phone once between cuts — but they stay separate tabs, because a client message costs a reply and an ops message can cost him money. Client rows carry the booking they're about; ops rows carry the case and its deadline. Ops threads can't be started here — they arrive. BMS-05 is the shop-side counterpart of the customer's payment case (CHT-07).
