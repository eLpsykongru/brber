T1Notifications — barber side

What actually interrupts a barber mid-cut. Three tiers: the lock screen and banners that arrive while his hands are busy (BNT-01), the in-app inbox grouped by day with actions in the row (BNT-02), and settings built around quiet hours and the one rule that matters — never buzz while someone's in the chair (BNT-03). Money and new requests push; everything else waits for the inbox.

T2A customer cancels — what Youssef gets

The other end of BKG-14. cancel_booking carries p_reason, and MyBookingsScreen already reads cancelled_by, cancel_reason — so the barber's job here is entirely about the hole in his day, not about being notified. Three surfaces: the push (BNT-04), the gap reopened on his timeline with something to do about it (BDY-06), and the pattern he can only see across bookings (BDY-07). The reason is shown as the customer's words, unedited and unrated — a shop that argues with cancellation reasons stops getting honest ones.

T3What “silent while cutting” swallowed

The barber half of gap G3. BNT-03 lets Youssef silence everything from check-in to mark-done, which is right — but a cancellation held for 41 minutes is a slot he could have resold. BNT-05 is the screen that hands the held ones over the moment he finishes, ordered by what is still worth doing, and offers the single exception rather than asking him to turn the rule off.
