DELDelete account — anonymised, not wiped

The server’s rule decides the screens. Removed: name, phone, email, photo, bio, ID document, portfolio photos, chat messages and photos. Kept without a name: bookings, reviews and every money record — statements and invoices are kept ten years by law. DEL-01 is a checklist of the four things that stand in the way, each with the one action that clears it; while any row is open the delete control isn’t drawn at all. A cleared row keeps its place with a green tick, so the list never reshuffles between two cuts. There is no shop transfer: an owner whose shop still has other barbers or an unpaid bill gets Talk to us, nothing more. DEL-02 says what goes and what stays in plain lines; the confirm word is the Latin DELETE in all three languages because the server checks it. DEL-03 is the signed-out end, with one line on why anything was kept.

MSGChat — unread you can count, only real actions

Unread is the one signal the product has, so it shows in three places: a count per thread, a badge on the Chat tab (sum of thread counts), and a New divider above the first unread message inside a thread. No read receipts, no online dots, no “typing…” — none exist. The phone icon in the header calls the client and is not drawn for a walk-in with no phone. The options sheet has three rows and nothing else. The composer is text and photo; the mic and emoji buttons are gone (the keyboard has its own emoji key).

EXPExport — one sheet, two doors

Wallet › Activity (BCF-01) and Owner › Shop report (ORP-01) both open this sheet; only the title and the PDF’s content differ. Pick a period, see the first page, hit Share PDF — the phone’s own share sheet takes over (WhatsApp, Mail, Files). No CSV, no email-it-to-me: the app prints PDFs already, and nothing else. The first page of each PDF is drawn full size beside the sheet; each carries only what its screen already shows.
