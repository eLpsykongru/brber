DELDelete account — anonymised, not wiped (redraw of PRO-09)

The old sheet promised to remove bookings and review history; under the new rule neither goes. Removed: name, phone, email, photo, date of birth, chat messages and photos, coupons. Kept without a name: bookings (barbers’ books still need them), reviews (shown as “Former customer”), money records (10 years, by law). The sheet becomes pushed screens because the honest version is longer. DEL-04 keeps today’s block on a live booking with a deposit. DEL-05 adds the wallet: money there can’t be paid out in cash, so the amount is named, spending it on a deposit is suggested, and a tick is required before the button arms. The confirm word is the Latin DELETE in all three languages.

MSGChat — real unread, no presence

Built on the current list (CHT-04, help pinned) with the All / Unread filter from CHT-01. Unread shows as a count on each thread, a badge on the Chat tab (sum of thread counts), real threads under the Unread filter with its own empty state, and a New divider inside a thread. Every online dot and the “Online” status are gone: presence doesn’t exist. The ⋮ sheet has two rows; there is no “Call the shop” because shops have no phone number yet. The composer is text and photo only. Two small fixes on the way: the pinned support row read “Rentra Support · Usually replies in 5 min” — the brand is Sterncut and nothing measures a reply time, so it now reads “Message the Sterncut team”.

SGNConsent at sign-up — one line, two real links

One line wherever an account can be created: under the three welcome buttons (SGN-01, redraw of AUTH-05) and in the register footer (SGN-02, redraw of AUTH-07). Both names are links that open sterncut.ma in the in-app browser. On the welcome screen the line was already there but its links weren’t tappable and it sat at 40 % white; it is now readable and each link has a 44 px tap area. Nothing else on either screen changes.
