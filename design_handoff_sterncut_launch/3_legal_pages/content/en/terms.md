## 1. What Sterncut is

> **In short —** A booking tool. Barbers and shops are independent: they do the work and get paid for it.

Sterncut is published by [legal entity], [legal form], RC [RC number], ICE [ICE number], with its registered office at [address].

Sterncut lets you find a shop, book a time, take a place in a queue and message a barber.

Barbers and shops are independent professionals. They set their services, prices and hours, and they alone are responsible for the service. Sterncut is not a party to the contract between you and the shop. **⚑1**

## 2. Your account

> **In short —** Accurate details and a password you keep to yourself.

You must be at least [minimum age] to create an account. **⚑2**

The details you give must be accurate. Your phone number is verified by SMS.

Keep your password secret. If you think someone else is using it, change it and write to us at [contact email].

## 3. Bookings and payment

> **In short —** The price is the shop's, shown before you book. You pay at the shop.

Services, prices and durations are set by the shop and shown in the app before you book.

You pay for the service at the shop. Apart from deposits, Sterncut takes no payment for services. **⚑3**

You can cancel a booking in the app. If the shop cancels, you're notified.

## 4. Deposits

> **In short —** Some shops ask for a deposit. The amount and the free-cancellation deadline are shown before you book. After that deadline, the deposit isn't refunded.

A shop can ask for a deposit to confirm a booking. The amount and the free-cancellation deadline are shown in the app before you confirm.

The deposit is paid from your Sterncut wallet. **⚑3**

- You cancel before the deadline: the deposit goes back to your wallet.
- You cancel after the deadline, or don't come: the deposit isn't refunded.
- The shop cancels: the deposit goes back to your wallet. **⚑4**
- You come: the deposit is taken off the price you pay at the shop. **⚑5**

## 5. No-shows and reliability

> **In short —** Not coming without cancelling counts as a no-show. Your no-shows and barbers' ratings can lead a shop to ask for a deposit.

If you don't come to a booking and haven't cancelled it, the shop can mark it as a no-show.

After each visit, the barber rates your reliability. Your no-shows and these ratings can lead a shop to ask you for a deposit on future bookings. **⚑6**

If a no-show was marked by mistake, write to us at [contact email]. **⚑7**

## 6. Reviews, reports and appeals

> **In short —** Reviews are public. We remove those that break these rules, and you can appeal our decisions.

After a visit, you can leave a review of the shop and the barber. **⚑8** Reviews are public.

A review must be about your visit. We remove reviews that are insulting, discriminatory, unrelated to the visit or that contain personal information. **⚑9**

Anyone can report a review, a message or a profile. If we remove your content or take action against your account, we tell you why, and you can appeal the decision in the app. **⚑10**

You allow us to display the reviews and photos you publish, free of charge, in the app and on sterncut.ma. Reviews stay displayed without your name if you delete your account. **⚑11**

## 7. Account suspension

> **In short —** We can suspend an account for fraud, abuse or a serious breach. We tell you why.

We can suspend or close an account for fraud, harassment, fake reviews, misuse of the wallet or repeated breaches of these terms. **⚑12**

Unless it's urgent, we warn you first and tell you why. You can appeal by writing to [contact email]. **⚑12**

## 8. Wallet

> **In short —** Topped up in cash at a shop, and nowhere else. The money can't be withdrawn.

The Sterncut wallet is topped up only in cash, at a shop that offers top-ups. Bank cards aren't accepted.

The balance is used to pay deposits. **⚑13** Deposit refunds, coupons and referral rewards are credited to it; their conditions are shown in the app.

The balance can't be withdrawn or refunded in cash. It's lost if you delete your account.

The wallet is not a bank account and earns no interest. **⚑14**

A shop subscribes to be bookable on Sterncut. The price is set per barber, monthly or yearly, with a maximum number of barbers billed, and is shown on sterncut.ma/tarifs and in the app before subscribing.

Commission or rent terms are agreed between the barber and the shop. Sterncut records them to produce statements, without being a party to that agreement. **⚑17**

Sterncut can't guarantee uninterrupted service. Sterncut isn't responsible for the quality of services, delays or disputes between a client and a shop, unless it is at fault. **⚑18**
