ANDAndroid — adaptive layers and notification icon

The three adaptive layers are 512 × 512 (108 dp). The dashed coral circle is the 66 dp safe zone (313 px); the grey one is the 72 dp area a launcher shows. The S sits inside the safe zone on the foreground and on the monochrome layer. Checkerboards mean transparent and aren’t part of the files.

SPLSplash — the mark on ink, and a light variant on canvas

The splash image is the mark alone, on transparent, drawn inside a centred circle two-thirds wide so Android 12’s round mask can’t clip it. Expo paints the background colour and shows the image at 200 dp wide. There’s no wordmark, loader or tagline.
